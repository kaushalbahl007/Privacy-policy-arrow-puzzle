Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Arrows Puzzle Escape" ("the app", "we") handles information when you play. It applies to the app on both iOS and Android. The app is free to play, supported by ads, and does not require an account.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your game progress (completed levels, stars, points, and coins) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, Google may automatically collect and process the following from your device:

  • Device or other IDs — your device's Advertising ID
  • Approximate location — estimated from your IP address (the app never uses GPS)
  • App interactions — such as ad views and clicks

This data is used solely for advertising and marketing purposes and is always transmitted encrypted. You can read how Google handles this data in the Google Privacy Policy and Google's Ad Policy.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, the app asks for your permission before your device's Advertising ID is used to personalise ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Your Choices

You can reset or delete your device's Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, at any time in your device settings:

  • On iOS — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising
  • On Android — Settings → Privacy → Ads

Notifications

The app may show local reminder notifications. These are generated entirely on your device and involve no data collection. You can turn them off any time in your device's notification settings.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We value your trust. The app itself sends no personal data anywhere, and all data transmitted by our advertising partner is encrypted in transit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
