Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "Arrows Puzzle Escape" ("the app", "we") handles information when you use the app. We do not collect personal data directly, and the app does not require you to create an account.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your game progress (completed levels, points, and coins) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

Third-Party Advertising

The app displays advertisements provided by Meta Audience Network (a service of Meta Platforms, Inc.). To serve and measure ads, Meta may automatically collect and process the following information from your device:

  • Device or other IDs — such as your device's Advertising ID
  • Approximate location — a coarse location estimated from your IP address (the app never accesses GPS)
  • App interactions — such as ad views and clicks within the app

This data is collected and shared with Meta solely for advertising and marketing purposes, and is transmitted using encryption. You can learn how Meta handles this data in the Meta Privacy Policy.

Your Choices

You can reset or delete your device's Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time from your device settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Notifications

The app may show local reminder notifications on your device. These are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any data collection. You can disable them any time in your device's notification settings.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We value your trust and strive to protect your information using reasonable safeguards. All data transmitted by the app's advertising partner is encrypted in transit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
